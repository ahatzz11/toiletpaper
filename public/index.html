How this works
  • Sheets divides the price by the total sheets in the pack. It's the simplest comparison, but sheet sizes differ a little between brands and lines.
  • Paper area divides by square feet of paper (sheets × sheet width × sheet length), so bigger sheets count for more.
  • Area × ply also counts a 3-ply square as 1.5 times a 2-ply square.
  • Roll sizes come from pack labels and can change. If your pack says something different, pick Custom and type in its numbers. Sources and notes.
