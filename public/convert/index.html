How this works
  • Sheets compares sheets per roll. Paper area compares square feet (sheets × sheet width × sheet length). Area × ply also counts a 3-ply square as 1.5 times a 2-ply square.
  • Each brand measures "= N regular rolls" against its own regular roll: 52 sheets at Charmin (worked out from the Mega pack) and 63 at Quilted Northern (printed on the pack).
  • An Honest Roll is 6 square feet of paper, about one regular roll from either brand.
  • Values tagged derived were computed from other printed numbers. estimated means not found printed. Sources and notes.
